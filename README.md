# E-Commerce Customer Analytics & RFM Segmentation

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer behavior, revenue performance, customer value, product performance, and market concentration using real-world e-commerce transaction data.

The project combines **Excel, Power Query, MySQL, SQL, Python, Statistics, and Power BI** to transform raw transaction data into actionable business insights.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

### Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce transaction data and answer key business questions:

- How is overall revenue performing?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- How frequently do customers purchase?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- Which products drive revenue and volume?
- How concentrated is revenue across products?
- Which markets contribute the most revenue?
- Where are the major business risks and growth opportunities?
